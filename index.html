Privacy Policy for Micomi

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Micomi uses Google Sign-In to create and manage user accounts. We collect only the information provided by Google Sign-In: your email address and basic profile information (such as name and profile photo, if available).

Information We Collect

  • Email address
  • Basic profile information from Google Sign-In (name, profile photo if available)
  • Purchase records (transaction ID and purchase status) if you buy items in the app

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and authenticate your account
  • To provide access to app features
  • To deliver purchased items or services

Payments

We use Google Play Billing to process purchases. We do not collect or store full payment card details. The payment provider handles your payment information and provides us with purchase records (such as transaction ID and purchase status) to deliver purchased items.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share data with service providers needed for authentication and payments (Google Sign-In and Google Play Billing).

Data Retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion at any time.
